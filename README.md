# Printer-ROI-Total-Cost-of-Ownership-TCO-Analysis
This financial model evaluates the long-term economic viability of three different printer models (Epsilon, HV, and Zero). The goal is to identify the most economical choice by factoring in both the initial capital expenditure (CAPEX) and the ongoing operational expenditure (OPEX) related to ink consumption.
How it Works
​The model uses a multi-layered formula structure to calculate the true cost of the asset:
​The Cost-Per-Page Engine
​Logic: Cost of Cartridge / Pages per Cartridge = Cost Per Page.
​The "Efficiency Gap": The model identifies that while the "Epsilon" is cheap to buy, its cost-per-page ($0.20) is 6.6x higher than the "Zero" model ($0.03).
​Longitudinal Forecasting
​The model projects costs over a 2-year period based on two user-defined scenarios:
​Low Volume: 15 pages per day.
​High Volume: 500 pages per day.
​Formula: (Pages per Year * Cost per Page * 2) + Purchase Price = Total Cost.
​Visualization & Analysis
​Comparative Bar Charts: Clearly illustrate the total financial liability for each printer.
​The "Scaling" Discovery: In the high-volume scenario (Tim), the model highlights a massive disparity:
​Epsilon (Budget): $50,029 total cost.
​Zero (Premium): $8,049 total cost.
​Technical Implementation
​Scenario Analysis: Used side-by-side data tables to compare disparate user profiles.
​UI/UX Design: High-contrast color-coding (Yellow for inputs, Red for final cost) to ensure data readability.
​Dynamic Linking: Adjusting the "Expected Pages Per Day" automatically recalibrates the 2-year forecast across all models.
​Strategic Value
​This framework is essential for businesses making procurement decisions, ensuring that long-term operational costs are prioritized over short-term savings.
